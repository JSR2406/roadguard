What was cross-validated?

Separate drawing geometry provides ground-truth boxes. The rule-based detector receives decoded pixels only. Predictions were then replayed through the authenticated API using one synthetic source identity: every cluster stayed provisional, two relevant route warnings were returned and the parallel route returned none. False positives were retained rather than discarded.

No collision, injury, death, physical depth, GPS localisation or physical camera independence was inferred. Trained ONNX inference requires a permitted trained model and independent real footage.
